Ne pas avoir ton téléphone sur toi quand tu es placé·e en garde à vue, c'est le meilleur moyen de protéger tes données et le mouvement. Powering it fully off is the second best approach.

<HowTo title="How to hand your phone to someone not risking arrest">
  * **If you're planning to risk arrest**: turn off your phone and hand it to your designated support person before deployment.
  * **If you need a phone during the action**: Folks in a support role (tactical, medic, de-escalation, etc) may need a phone during the action. Depending on the action design, and the unpredictability of the cops, they may or may not be able to leave when arrest risk goes up.
    * If your action design allows it and you need your phone up until the last minute, hand it off.
    * If you need your phone and you can't hand it off, make sure you fully power it off before arrest.
</HowTo>
