[Citizen Lab found](https://citizenlab.ca/research/how-discover-tech-turned-targeted-ads-into-a-pipeline-for-spyware/) spyware being delivered through targeted ads shown inside everyday apps and websites. Just loading the ad was enough to infect a device, even if you never saw it or clicked on it.

Blocking ads closes off this path. It also makes it harder for cops and data brokers to [track your location through ad networks](/vpn/#value).

<HowTo title="How to block ads">
  <InlineChecklist>
    * **Install a [trusted VPN with ad-blocking](/vpn/#recommended) on your phone and keep it on.** This is the most important step because it blocks ads inside your apps, not just in your browser. In IVPN, turn on **AntiTracker**. In Proton VPN, turn on **NetShield** (paid plans only). See [how to set up a VPN](/vpn/#how).
    * **Install the same VPN on your computer** and turn on ad-blocking there too.
    * **Use a [privacy-focused browser](/essentials/#browser) that blocks ads**, like Brave or Firefox with [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/). This only protects what you do in that browser.
  </InlineChecklist>

  *If you don't want to use a VPN,* an ad-blocking DNS service like [AdGuard](https://adguard.com/en/welcome.html) can block ads across your phone instead. See [encrypted DNS](/vpn/#dns).
</HowTo>
