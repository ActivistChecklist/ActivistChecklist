title: ابحث يدويًا على Google عن معلوماتك الخاصة
preview: |

---

إذا اعتمدت فقط على أدوات إزالة البيانات التلقائية مثل EasyOptOuts، فعلى الأرجح ستظل هناك نتائج كثيرة تكشف معلوماتك الخاصة.

أهم ما يشغلنا هو إزالة عنوان منزلك الحالي، ثم رقم هاتفك في المرتبة الثانية. قد يكون بريدك الإلكتروني منشورًا ويعرّضك للتحرش، لكن التعامل معه أسهل.

يمكن أن يساعدك [تطبيق Kanary](https://www.kanary.com/) (النسخة المجانية) في جزء من عملية البحث هذه.

<HowTo title="كيف تجد معلوماتك الشخصية على Google وتزيلها">
  <StepList>
    1. **Do separate Google searches for your info.** Use quotes around your search to get more accuracy. Scroll through the first 2–3 pages on Google for each search (don't use Brave Search or other privacy search engines for this task).
       * **Home address in quotes:** `"742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield"` or even just `"742 Evergreen Tr"` though that may turn up too many results that aren't in your city.
       * **Phone number in quotes:** `"555-867-5309"` or `"5558675309"` or `"(555) 867-5309"`
       * **Full name plus city (each in separate quotes):** `"Jamie Garcia" "Springfield"`
       * **Just your full name in quotes:** `"Jamie Garcia"` (will produce a lot of results that might not be you)
    2. **Keep a list** of what you find that includes your sensitive private info. Prioritize sites that list your address or phone number.
    3. **Make a plan to get each one removed.**
       * If it's a "people search" site, look for a "do not sell my info" or "opt out" form to fill out.
       * If it's a social media site, the author needs to remove it. Or you can try to press "report content" if they're unwilling to remove it.
       * If it's some other site, look for a contact form and ask them to remove it because you're concerned about being the target of online attacks. Most sites are willing to help.
       * **Ask search engines to remove the result.** For harassment, doxxing, or other harmful content, you can submit a removal request directly to Google ([form](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/9673730?hl=en)) and to Bing ([form](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/tools/contentremoval)). Success rates vary, but it's worth trying for the most harmful results.
    4. **Set a reminder to check back in.** Check in after a week. If you didn't hear back from any of these sites, try to follow up.
    5. **Set up** [**Google Alerts**](https://www.google.com/alerts) for each search above so you're notified when new items come in. (Google's "Results About You" is good but it definitely misses things.)
    6. **Refresh the Google results.** If you got a listing removed on the source site, but it's still showing up in Google's results, you can [request the content be updated](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7041154?hl=en).
    7. **Try other search engines too.** Different search engines surface different results, and an attacker isn't limited to Google. Run the same searches on Bing and DuckDuckGo to see what else turns up.
  </StepList>
</HowTo>
