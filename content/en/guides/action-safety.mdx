**Who this guide is for:** Organizers of a civil disobedience action that involves risking arrest. These actions are publicly visible, nonviolent, and usually come with low-level charges like trespass, or failure to disperse. Examples: sit-in, blockading a building or street. 

*If you're doing an action that carries higher legal consequences, you should look for more specialized resources and support beyond what’s offered in this document.*

<Alert type="success">
  <Collapsible title="Attending an action?" className="my-0">
    *If you're attending an action rather than organizing one, here are a few quick tips if you only have a few minutes to prepare:*

    <InlineChecklist>
      * **Don’t bring your phone if you’re risking arrest**. Turn it off if your risk of arrest increases. (Or give it to an assigned support person who will get it back to you.)
      * **Don’t talk to the cops** or cooperate in government repression
      * **Have a buddy** keep track of each other
      * **Know the “magic words”:** Am I being detained? Am I free to go? I do not consent to a search. I want to speak to my lawyer. I am going to remain silent.
    </InlineChecklist>

    See more tips for [protest digital security](/protest/).
  </Collapsible>
</Alert>

## Top steps for organizers {#top-steps}

Here are the most important steps from this guide:

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Talk to a movement lawyer** so everyone understands the range of [legal risks](#legal) they are agreeing to and knows how to assert their rights.
  * **Make a phone plan:** phones [stay home or with someone](/action/) who is not risking arrest.
  * **Fill the support roles:** on-site jail support, off-site jail support, police liaison, a safety team. Possibly a medic.
  * **Align on your action security agreements:** Nonviolence, don’t talk to the cops or cooperate with state repression, only share plans with people who need to know, and don’t write down anything you wouldn’t want read in court.
</InlineChecklist>

## Your group & risk {#risk-assessment}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Form an [affinity group](https://www.seedsforchange.org.uk/downloads/affinitygroups.pdf)** with trusted 
  * **Discuss the risk tolerances of each person in your group.**

    Design your action with these risk tolerances in mind. We want to aim to take strategic risk and reduce unnecessary risk.
  * **Do a risk assessment:** 
   
    Assess the likelihood of various risks based on your action design and your local conditions. [Risk mapping](https://riskmapper.app/) with others can help you assess what risks actually need your attention (as opposed to fears).
</InlineChecklist>

## Digital security {#digital-security}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Use Signal** and make sure everyone has [disappearing messages turned on by default](/action/#signal-disappearing) for all new threads.
  * **Develop your [phones plan](/action/):**

    Anyone risking arrest should either A) do not bring your phone or B) have one person collect phones (powered off) from those risking arrest and return them after arrests. For those with phones, turn off any remaining phones if your arrest risk is going up.
  * **Don't [write things down](/action/#signal-disappearing)** that you don’t want to have read back to you in court. (Even on Signal)
</InlineChecklist>

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Recruit a [safety/de-escalation team](https://visionchangewin.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/VCW-Safety-Toolkit-Final.pdf).**

  * **Make sure everyone has a buddy**

## Legal support & know your rights {#legal}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Find a movement-aligned lawyer and consult them about your action.**

    Places to look: [local NLG chapter](https://www.nlg.org/chapters/), [NLG National](https://www.nlg.org/), [Movement Law Lab](https://www.movementlawlab.org/), or [CLDC](https://cldc.org/). See [questions to ask your group and an attorney about legal risks](https://ccrjustice.org/sites/default/files/attach/2024/11/Protest%20Planner%202024.pdf) (Center for Constitutional Rights).
  * **Do a legal briefing** with a movement-aligned lawyer and anyone in your group so everyone understands the range of risks and knows how to assert their rights.
</InlineChecklist>

## Arrests, jail support, & police liaison {#jail-support}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Recruit a [jail support](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yVVW7_N47PKp_OW5Ge7K2452vB9Ar5BIbTcFsZXRMC8/edit?tab=t.0) team** (at least one on-site and one off-site person)
  * **Collect jail support forms:**
  
    For coordinated, pre-planned actions, collect jail support forms for anyone risking arrest. ([Example 1](https://www.punchupcollective.org/faq-the-role-of-police-liaison/), [Example 2](https://www.thursdaybram.com/zines/jail%20support%20form.pdf), [Example 3](https://rosehipmedics.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Jail-Support-Form-Only-2-pages.pdf)). Collecting on paper is best, but [Cryptpad](https://cryptpad.fr/) forms can work if you need digital.
  * **Pick a phone number folks can call from jail if needed.**

    This can be a [local NLG hotline](https://www.nlg.org/chapters/) (if you’ve arranged it with them!) or just someone in your group who is off-site. Make sure each person writes it on their body with a sharpie.
  * **Destroy the jail support info** after the action.
</InlineChecklist>

## Vetting, need-to-know, & security culture {#security-culture}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Develop action agreements.** ([Example 1](https://stopthemoneypipeline.org/how-we-act/), [Example 2](https://counteract.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/participants-agreement-sample-2023.pdf))
  * **Create a vetting or [vouching](https://www.thedirectactionmovement.com/vouching) process.**
  * **Create need-to-know agreements** 
    
    Don't share information with anyone outside of your group who doesn't need to know. Don't talk about the action before or after with anyone who wasn't involved.
  * **Practice solidarity [against state repression](https://nspm7union.com/defendants-pledge/)** 

    Under this authoritarian state, sharing names of colleagues has been used to expand surveillance and transparently showing signal threads has led to pulling out jokes as if they were serious threats in courtrooms. Do not talk to the cops, do not give them access to data or information, do not become a cooperating witness. This is how we keep each other safe.
</InlineChecklist>
