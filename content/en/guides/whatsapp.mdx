A lot of activism is already happening on WhatsApp, especially in immigrant communities. It isn't the most secure or private choice, but it's sometimes a useful tool for community organizing because people are already using it or requires features offered by WhatsApp (like Communities).

Your WhatsApp messages are [end-to-end encrypted](term:end-to-end-encryption), which makes WhatsApp more secure than regular text messages for protecting what you say. If the cops subpoena your phone carrier for your regular texts, they can see the content of every message. If they subpoena Meta for your WhatsApp messages, they cannot see the content (as long as you don't have Chat Backups enabled).

<Alert type="warning">
  **Think of doing large group organizing WhatsApp the same way you would social media:** Assume that bad actors (government, right-wing) could join and monitor any large-unvetted group just like they could join and monitor large groups on social media. Assume they can also see who is in the group in the same way they could see who is following you on social media.
</Alert>

Even with the protections in this guide, there are a number of things WhatsApp will always be able to turn over to law enforcement:

* Your entire contact list (if you have ever given the app access to your contact list, they will always have it, even if you disable it later)
* All the other WhatsApp users who have YOU in their contact list ([source](https://www.justsecurity.org/79549/we-now-know-what-information-the-fbi-can-obtain-from-encrypted-messaging-apps/))
* The names of all groups you are in
* The members of all the groups you're in
* Which phone numbers you're messaging and when (they can even get this information in [real-time](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/whatsapp-imessage-facebook-apple-fbi-privacy-1261816/), which most other tech companies don't provide to law enforcement)
* If you enable chat backups, Google or Apple can be compelled to turn them over, and they contain all of your messages. See [Securely back up your WhatsApp chats](/whatsapp/#whatsapp-secure-backup) for how to use backups safely.


**Brand new to WhatsApp?** See this guide on [how to use WhatsApp](https://ssd.eff.org/module/how-to-use-whatsapp).
