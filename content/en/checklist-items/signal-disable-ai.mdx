---
title: 'Disable AI notification summaries'
---
Google and Apple and Microsoft are all integrating AI features across their platforms in a way that can put your Signal messages at risk of being sent off your device to their servers. Notification summaries are one place where your messages data gets sent off device.

We wrote a separate article on this topic here: [Is Apple Intelligence reading my Signal messages?](/signal-ai/)

Signal has also implemented [some protections against other AI features](https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/) that read your screen.

<HowTo title="Option A: How to disable AI notification summaries just for Signal">
  #### Option A: Disable AI notification summaries just for Signal

  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* Open the **Settings** app > **Notifications** > **Summarize Notifications** > Toggle off **Signal**</MenuPath>

  <MenuPath>*Android:* **Settings** > **Notifications** > **Notification summaries** > **Manage apps** (under **Don't include these apps**) > Toggle off **Signal**</MenuPath>

</HowTo>

<HowTo title="How to entirely disable AI notification summaries">
  #### Option B: Entirely disable AI notification summaries

  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* **Settings** > **Notifications** > Toggle off **Summarize Notifications **</MenuPath>

  <MenuPath>*Android:* Open **Settings** > **Notifications** > Toggle off **Use notification summaries** (or **Notification summaries**)</MenuPath>

  <MenuPath>*Mac:*  > **System Settings** > **Apple Intelligence & Siri** > Toggle off **Apple Intelligence** > **Confirm**</MenuPath>

  *(Mac requires an Apple computer with an M1 processor or newer.)*
</HowTo>
