For lower-risk arrestable actions, it's best to have everyone either A) leave their phone at home or B) **turn off their phones** and leave them with a support person. This is often a person doing [jail support](term:jail-support). This person should either not attend the action or be in a very low-risk position on the far edges of the action. 

<HowTo title="How to set a phone agreement for your action">
  1. **Decide which roles actually need a phone.** Usually this is only a few people, like the tactical lead, police liaison, or medics. Everyone else leaves their phones with the support person. Anyone in a high arrest risk role should not bring a phone. (Or they should immediately hand it over after deployment.)
  2. **Before deployment, the support person collects most phones.**
  3. **Make a plan for getting phones back to people after they are released**. This usually means the jail support person brings the phones to people after they are released.
  4. **Share the agreement early and repeat it** at trainings and the action briefing.
</HowTo>

**Tip**: If there are a lot of phones and you need to confirm each person is the true owner of the phone they point to in the stack, you can ask them to unlock the phone in your presence.
