title: Disable bluetooth and wifi
preview: >
  Cops can use [tracking
  tools](https://www.wired.com/story/dnc-hidden-signal-hunt/) to identify who is
  in an area based on the bluetooth and wifi signals your phone is emitting.
---
If you're bringing your phone, we assume you're using it to communicate and need a cell connection, and that turning on airplane mode probably won't work for you. Given that, it's best to **disable bluetooth and wifi** to reduce tracking.

<Alert type="warning">
  **iPhone Warning:** Swiping down and using "**control center**" to disable bluetooth/wifi doesn't actually turn it off. It just disconnects you from current networks, but it is still transmitting signals that can be tracked. To fully turn it off, you need to follow the instructions below.
</Alert>

<HowTo title="How to disable bluetooth and wifi">
  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* **Settings** > **Bluetooth** > **Disable Bluetooth**</MenuPath>

  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* **Settings** > **Wi-Fi** > **Disable Wi-Fi**</MenuPath>

  <MenuPath>*Android:* **Settings** > **Bluetooth** > **Advanced** (or three-dot menu) > **Disable Bluetooth** and **turn off Bluetooth scanning**</MenuPath>

  <MenuPath>*Android:* **Settings** > **Wi-Fi** > **Advanced** (or three-dot menu) > **Disable Wi-Fi** and **turn off Scanning** or **Wi-Fi scanning**</MenuPath>
</HowTo>
