Create household protocols for handling unexpected visitors to prevent intrusion and keep everyone safer.

> ***Proposed basic door-answering practices***
>
> 1. **You don't have to open the door:** You can talk through the door or not answer at all
> 2. **If it's law enforcement or government agents:** Do not open the door unless they have a [judicialwarrant](term:warrant-subpoena). Ask to see the warrant through a window or have them slide it under the door
> 3. **If they claim to have a warrant:** Ask them to clearly state their name, agency, and the purpose of their visit before opening
> 4. **Never invite agents inside:** Even if you open the door, you can step outside and close the door behind you
> 5. **Always check who's at the door before opening:** Use a peephole, window, or doorbell camera if available

<HowTo title="How to establish door-answering practices">
  1. Share the ["If An Agent Knocks"](https://docs.google.com/document/d/176Yds1p63Q3iaKilw0luChMzlJhODdiPvF2I4g9eIXo/edit?usp=sharing) resource from Center for Constitutional Rights with others in your household
  2. Establish household rules about verifying identity before opening door. See template above.
  3. Share door-answering protocols with all household members and discuss (see discussion agenda below)
  4. Rehearse what you may say to unexpected visitors (deliveries you didn't order, etc.)
</HowTo>

**Script for talking to household members unfamiliar with this context:**

> I want to talk about what we'd do if cops ever knock on our door. Because of my organizing work, there's a chance that could happen, so I want us to be prepared just in case. (This doesn't mean you need to get involved in my organizing.)
>
> Here's what I'm hoping we can all agree on:
>
> * We don't open the door unless they have a warrant.
> * If one of us does open the door, we step outside, close it behind us, and say "I do not consent to a search."
> * We don't talk to the cops, even if they say they "just want to talk." We can say "I am going to remain silent" and "I want to speak to an attorney."
> * If someone says they're from a company we didn't call, or has a delivery we didn't order, we check who it is before opening the door.
>
> Can you take a few minutes to look over this poster: [https://activistchecklist.org/police](https://activistchecklist.org/police)
> I'm happy to sit down and practice what we'd say together if you have time this week.
