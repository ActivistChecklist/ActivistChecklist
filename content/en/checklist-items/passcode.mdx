<HowTo title="How to change your passcode">
  <StepList>
    1. **Select a random passcode that is 8 to 10 digits long.** The most secure way to generate a random passcode is to use your password manager's random password generator feature (set it to numbers only), but you can also use our passcode generator. (Humans are bad at making up random passcodes, so we suggest not trying to think of one yourself.)

       <PasscodeGenerator />
    2. Change your passcode:

        <MenuPath>*iPhone:* **Settings** > **Face ID & Passcode** > **Change Passcode** > **Passcode Options** > **Custom Numeric Code**</MenuPath>

       <MenuPath>*Android:* **Settings** > **Security** > **Screen Lock** > Enter Current Lock > **PIN/Password** > Enter a Passcode</MenuPath>
    3. **Practice the new passcode** at least 10 times in a row right now so you are more likely to remember it. (Disabling face/fingerprint unlock will force you to enter the passcode every time you open your phone.)
    4. Write your new passcode on paper and keep it somewhere safe at home until you've memorized it. Then destroy it after 2–3 weeks. Setting a reminder on your phone can help.
  </StepList>
</HowTo>

<Collapsible title="For extra security, use a stopwatch to pick your random digits">
  Using any online passcode generator (even ours) carries some small risks. If you want the highest level of security, you can use the stopwatch feature on your phone. 
  
  1. Start the stopwatch and stop it without looking at the screen.
  2. Write down the last digit (the hundredths of a second).
  3. Repeat the process until you have 8 to 10 digits.
</Collapsible> 

**How long does it take to crack a passcode?**

| Type                                | Time it takes to crack (average) | Example                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 6-digit ***easy-to-guess*** pattern | **Less than 24 hours** to crack  | *333666 (common pattern)* or *110582 (date pattern for Nov 5, 1982)* |
| 6-digit ***random*** code           | **200 days** to crack            | *238253*                                                               |
| 8-digit ***random*** code           | **40+ years** to crack           | *34780026*                                                             |

*Note*: These times only apply to phones. Computers can be cracked much more quickly, and need much stronger passwords.

*Sources:* The estimates in the table above assume real-world observed attempts/second from police forensic hacking tools. If you need more security, use a 10-digit passcode, which will protect you even under the highest-possible cracking scenarios. See the sources linked in the [passcode FAQ here](https://strongphrase.net/passcode).
