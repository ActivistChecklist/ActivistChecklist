---
title: After the action, delete the planning docs and Signal groups
preview: |
  Old chats and docs can still be read if a phone is seized or an account is subpoenaed later.
firstPublished: 2026-10-30
lastUpdated: 2026-10-30
---
Once the action is over, the documents and chats you made are no longer needed. The longer they stick around, the more chances that they can end up in the hands of the cops through a seized phone, a [subpoena](term:warrant-subpoena), or an informant in the group.

**Before the action**, agree on a cleanup plan. Create an accountability system to help you follow through.

<Alert type="warning">
  **If anyone was arrested or charged, check with your legal support team or a lawyer before deleting anything.** Deleting records once you know about an investigation, subpoena, or charges can be treated as destroying evidence, which is a separate crime. This is another reason to turn on [disappearing messages with a short timer](/action/#signal-disappearing) before the action, so most messages are already gone.
</Alert>

<HowTo title="How to clean up after an action">
  1. **Delete the documents.** This includes planning docs, Proton/CryptPad docs, spreadsheets, jail support forms, and any written notes. Then empty the trash.
  2. **Shut down the Signal groups**: Signal groups can be useful for ongoing organizing but the list of members also presents a risk to everyone if anyone's phone gets seized in the future. Consider moving everyone into a bigger announcement-only group for ongoing organizing rather than keeping that one small group going.
     * Post a message letting everyone know the group is closing, and ask them to delete the chat from their phones. Remove each member yourself to make sure it happens.
     * Delete the chat from your own phone: <MenuPath inline>Signal > Main screen > Swipe left on the thread > **Delete**</MenuPath>. (This won't remove the group from other people's phones, just yours. Disappearing messages will auto-delete from everyone's phones, though.)
  3. **If you're not an admin,** [leave AND delete the group](/action/#signal-leave-extra-groups) and encourage everyone else to do the same.
  4. **Shred any paper notes**, maps, or lists.
</HowTo>
