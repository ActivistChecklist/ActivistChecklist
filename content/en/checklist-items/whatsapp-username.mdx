WhatsApp now lets you pick a username. Once your username is active, people who don't already have your phone number saved see your username instead of your number. You can also give someone your username instead of your phone number when you want to connect with them.

This matters most in large or unvetted organizing groups, which can be infiltrated by bad actors. Your phone number is one of the easiest ways for someone to [find out who you are](/doxxing/).

WhatsApp is turning usernames on country by country. If usernames aren't active where you live yet, you can still reserve one now so it's ready when they are.

<HowTo title="How to set up a username">
  <MenuPath>WhatsApp > **Settings** > **Account** > **Username** > pick a username</MenuPath>

  Pick a username that you don't use anywhere else and that doesn't include your real name. If you reuse a handle from Instagram, X, or another site, anyone can connect your WhatsApp account to that profile.
</HowTo>

<HowTo title="How to add a username key">
  A username key is an optional extra code. Someone has to know both your username and your key to message you for the first time. This makes it harder for strangers to guess your username and contact you.

  <MenuPath>WhatsApp > **Settings** > **Account** > **Username** > **Username key**</MenuPath>

  If you start getting unwanted messages, reset your key. People who already chat with you aren't affected.
</HowTo>

<Alert type="warning">
  A username doesn't make you anonymous.

  * You still need a phone number to use WhatsApp, and Meta can still hand it over to law enforcement.
  * Anyone who already has your number saved, and anyone you've already chatted with by phone number, can still see it.
  * Meta still knows who you talk to and which groups you're in.
</Alert>
