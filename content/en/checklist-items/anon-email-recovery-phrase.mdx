When you create an anonymous email account, the provider offers you a unique recovery phrase. It works as a password reset tool in case your password is lost or forgotten.

An anonymous account has no other safety net. The usual password reset options (a phone number or a backup email address) would expose your identity, so you should not add them. That leaves the recovery phrase as your only way back in.

<Alert type="info" title="Use a password manager">
  A [password manager](/essentials/#password-manager) is the safest place to keep the recovery phrase. Don't email it to yourself, don't keep it in a notes app.
</Alert>
