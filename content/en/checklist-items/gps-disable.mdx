Apps on your phone collect your precise location and sell it to [data brokers](term:data-broker). Cops buy this data without a warrant, using tools like [Webloc](https://www.404media.co/inside-ices-tool-to-monitor-phones-in-entire-neighborhoods/) to see which phones were in an area, including at a protest. Location history saved by Google or Apple can also be subpoenaed.

Disabling location services on your whole phone stops apps from collecting your GPS location, so it can't be sold to data brokers or accessed by Google/Apple.

<Alert type="warning">
  **Disabling location services does not hide your location from your cell carrier.** Whenever your phone is on and has cell service, your carrier can tell roughly where you are from the towers you connect to, and cops can get that data. From most to least protective:

  1. Leave your phone at home.
  2. Turn your phone off. A Faraday bag adds even more protection.
  3. Turn on airplane mode **and** disable location services.
  4. Disable location services only.

  Airplane mode alone doesn't turn off GPS. Apps can still record your location and upload it once you reconnect.
</Alert>

<HowTo title="How to disable location services">
  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* **Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Disable Location Services**</MenuPath>

  *(Quicker shortcut: Say to Siri "Disable location tracking")*

  <MenuPath>*Android:* **Settings** > **Location** > **Disable Use Location**</MenuPath>

  *Other tips:*

  * You might want to **print maps** or **save offline maps** for navigation.
  * If you have any smartwatches or bluetooth "lost my keys/wallet" trackers, make sure to leave them at home or disable tracking there as well.
</HowTo>
