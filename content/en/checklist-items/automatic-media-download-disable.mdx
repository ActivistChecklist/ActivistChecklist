---
title: Turn off automatic media downloads on Signal and WhatsApp
---
When someone sends you a photo or video, your phone often downloads it automatically. Even though you haven't opened it, your device is processing that file to generate a thumbnail or preview. Some spyware attacks have been delivered through WhatsApp using images (or files) that exploit bugs in how your phone processes the images. 

(Note: There has never been an example of Signal being exploited in the same way, but you can disable it in Signal as well if you want be very cautious.)

<HowTo title="How to disable automatic media downloads">
  * **WhatsApp:** <MenuPath inline>**Settings** > **Storage and Data** > **Media auto-download** > Set every option to **No media** (Android) or **Never** (iPhone)</MenuPath>
  * **Signal:** <MenuPath inline>Open Signal > Tap your profile picture > **Settings** > **Data and Storage** (Android) or **Data Usage** (iPhone) > **Media auto-download** > **Uncheck everything**</MenuPath>
</HowTo>
