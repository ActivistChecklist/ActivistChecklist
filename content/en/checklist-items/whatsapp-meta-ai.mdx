Activists should consider Meta AI dangerous just like all of Meta's products. They want to collect your data and use it to sell you ads. Anything you ask it will get stored and could be turned over to law enforcement.

**Tagging @MetaAI in a group chat pulls the surrounding messages into Meta's systems**, even if you only meant to ask a quick question. Once Meta AI is in the conversation, that history is no longer just between the people in the chat.

<Alert type="warning">
  You can't entirely disable Meta AI in WhatsApp. The best protection is to never use it, and to never tag **@MetaAI** in a group chat.
</Alert>

To keep Meta AI out of specific chats and groups, also turn on [Restricted chat](/whatsapp/#whatsapp-advanced-chat-privacy) on those chats.
