Restricted chat is a per-chat setting. When it's on, nobody in that chat can:

* Export the chat
* Have photos and videos from the chat automatically saved to their camera roll
* Use Meta AI features in the chat
* Open the chat on a linked device, like WhatsApp Web, WhatsApp Desktop, or a tablet

<Alert type="info">
  **A restricted chat won't show up on your computer.** Once it's on, you and everyone else in the chat can only read and send messages in it from your main phone. The chat won't be available in WhatsApp Desktop, WhatsApp Web, or on a tablet, for anyone in the chat.
</Alert>

That trade-off is the point. Linked devices are how AI agents and unofficial WhatsApp apps get into people's chats. If one person in your group connects an AI assistant or a third-party app to their WhatsApp account, it can read every chat that account can see, including yours. Restricted chat keeps those tools out of that chat. It also protects the chat if an attacker tricks someone into linking a device to their account (see [Check WhatsApp's Linked Devices list regularly](/whatsapp/#whatsapp-linked-devices)).

You must turn it on for each sensitive chat. It can't be set automatically across all chats, and you can only change it from your main phone.

<HowTo title="How to turn on Restricted chat">
  **For a one-on-one chat** (either person can turn it on):

  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* Open the chat > tap the contact's name at the top > **Restricted chat** > toggle on</MenuPath>

  <MenuPath>*Android:* Open the chat > ⋮ > **View contact** > **Restricted chat** > toggle on</MenuPath>

  **For a group** (only group admins can turn it on):

  <MenuPath>Open the group > tap the group name at the top > **Restricted chat** > toggle on</MenuPath>

  If you're not an admin, ask an admin of the group to turn it on.
</HowTo>

<HowTo title="If you already turned on Advanced Chat Privacy">
  Restricted chat replaces an older setting called Advanced Chat Privacy. Chats where you turned on Advanced Chat Privacy **won't switch to Restricted chat automatically**, and they can still be opened on linked devices until you upgrade them.

  <MenuPath>Open the chat > tap the chat name at the top > **Advanced chat privacy** > **Upgrade** > **Continue**</MenuPath>
</HowTo>

<Alert type="warning">
  Restricted chat only fully works if everyone in the chat is on the latest version of WhatsApp. Someone on an older version may still be able to read the chat on a linked device, an unofficial app, or an AI agent, and WhatsApp won't tell you. Ask everyone in sensitive groups to keep WhatsApp updated. It also doesn't stop anyone from taking screenshots or forwarding individual messages.
</Alert>
