---
title: Log out of websites that have sensitive information (phone and computer)
preview: >
  If you're not signed in, there's less for border agents to see (they're only supposed to search what's stored on your device)
---
CBP's [policy](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/cbp_directive_3340-049b_jan_2026_508.pdf) says border agents can only search what's stored on your device. They are supposed to put your phone in airplane mode first, and they can't use it to look at things that are only stored online. So if you're logged out of your accounts, there's much less for them to see.

Logging out may not delete everything (temporary cached files can sometimes still live on your device), but it can help.

<HowTo title="Which websites to log out">
  Remember to log out on both your phone *<u>**and**</u>* your laptop.

  Websites to log out of:

  * Email and calendar (work and personal)
  * Docs (Google Drive, etc)
  * Social media
  * Discussion forums (Reddit, etc)

  And anything else you wouldn't want the cops looking at.
</HowTo>
