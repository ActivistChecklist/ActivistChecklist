We recommend **Brave** because it offers the most ad-blocking and privacy without any additional configuration. (Making things easy to use for most folks is one of the goals of this site.) The crypto features in Brave are annoying and stupid, but you can ignore/disable them.

Firefox is also a great choice but requires [additional configuration](https://www.privacyguides.org/en/desktop-browsers/#firefox) to get the [ad-blocking](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/ublock-origin/) and privacy benefits. Use [**Tor Browser**](/research/) for highly sensitive browsing that is truly anonymous.

Blocking advertisements matters because ads lead to [data brokers](term:data-broker) collecting information on you, which is then sold to companies that [the cops use to track your location and identity](https://www.404media.co/inside-ices-tool-to-monitor-phones-in-entire-neighborhoods/). Ads are also being used as a way to get [spyware onto the phones of dissidents](https://www.wired.com/story/nightmare-scenario-zero-click-spyware-is-infecting-phones-via-targeted-ads/).

<HowTo title="How to set up Brave Browser">
  Brave is a privacy-focused browser that allows you to install Google Chrome extensions.

  <StepList>
    1. [**Install Brave**](https://brave.com/download/) on your computer (or [phone](https://brave.com/download/#brave-mobile)).
    2. Follow the steps after you launch to import your configuration from Chrome or [another browser](https://support.brave.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019782291-How-do-I-import-or-export-browsing-data). (See warning below about how plugins make you more identifiable.)
    3. **Configure privacy settings:** On the desktop browser, go to <MenuPath inline>Brave > **Settings** > **Shields**</MenuPath> then select the following: (The mobile app will not have all these settings)
       * Select **Aggressive** under "Trackers & ads blocking"
       * Select **Strict** under "Upgrade connections to HTTPS"
       * Uncheck everything under **Social media blocking**
       * (Optional) Enable **Forget me when I close this site.** (On the mobile app, the setting is called "Shred Site Data") The site won't be able to store anything about you after you reset your browser.

         This will make it harder for sites to track you across the internet. It's good for privacy, but you'll want to manually override this for specific sites. <MenuPath inline>Visit the site > Click the Brave (lion) logo in the URL bar > **Advanced controls** > **Disable Forget me when I close this site**</MenuPath>
  </StepList>

  **Optional:**

  1. Disable the annoying new tab page: <MenuPath inline>Brave > **Settings** > **Get started** > **New Tab Page** > Select **Blank page** from the dropdown</MenuPath>
  2. Disable toolbar items: <MenuPath inline>Brave > **Settings** > **Appearance** > **Toolbar** > Disable all the toolbar buttons that you don't want (Brave Rewards, VPN, Wallet, Leo AI, etc)</MenuPath>
</HowTo>

**Bonus Brave configuration tips:**

* Install [Privacy Badger](https://privacybadger.org/) for some added protection.
* You can install the [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brave-private-web-browser-vpn/id1052879175) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brave.browser\&hl=en) Brave app as well.

<Alert type="warning">
  **Plugins warning:** Every plugin you install makes your browser stand out from “the crowd” and makes you more identifiable, reducing the effectiveness of the privacy features built-in to Brave.
</Alert>
