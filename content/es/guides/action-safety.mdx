**Who this guide is for:** Organizers of civil disobedience actions where people risk arrest. These actions are public, nonviolent, and usually lead to low-level charges like trespassing or failure to disperse. Examples include sit-ins and blockades of buildings or streets.

*If your action carries higher legal consequences, look for more specialized resources and support than this guide offers.*

<Alert type="success">
  <Collapsible title="¿Vas a ir a una acción?" className="my-0">
    *If you’re attending rather than organizing and only have a few minutes to prepare, start here:*

    <InlineChecklist>
      * **Don’t bring your phone if you’re risking arrest.** If you do bring it, turn it off when your risk of arrest goes up, or give it to an assigned support person who will get it back to you.
      * **Don’t talk to the cops** or cooperate with government repression.
      * **Have a buddy** and keep track of each other.
      * **Conoce las “palabras mágicas”:** ¿Estoy detenide? ¿Me puedo ir? No doy mi consentimiento para un registro. Quiero hablar con mi abogade. Voy a permanecer en silencio.
    </InlineChecklist>

    For more tips, see our [protest digital security checklist](/protest/).
  </Collapsible>
</Alert>

## Top steps for action organizers {#top-steps}

Here are the most important steps from this guide:

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Talk to a movement lawyer** so everyone understands the range of [legal risks](#legal) they are agreeing to and knows how to assert their rights.
  * **Make a plan to secure phones:** they should either [stay home or with someone](/action/) who is not risking arrest.
  * **Fill the support roles:** on-site and off-site jail support, a police liaison, a safety team, and possibly medics and legal observers.
  * **Align on your security agreements:** nonviolence, not talking to the cops or cooperating with state repression, sharing plans only with people who need to know, and not writing down anything you wouldn’t want read in court.
</InlineChecklist>

## Your group & risk {#risk}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Form an [affinity group](https://www.seedsforchange.org.uk/downloads/affinitygroups.pdf)** with trusted folks.
  * **Hablen sobre cuánto riesgo está dispuesta a asumir cada persona del grupo.**

    Diseña tu acción teniendo en cuenta estos niveles de tolerancia al riesgo. Aim to take strategic risks and avoid unnecessary ones.
  * **Do a risk assessment.**

    Assess how likely different risks are, based on your action design and local conditions. [Risk mapping](https://riskmapper.app/) with others can help you separate the risks that need your attention from fears that don’t.
</InlineChecklist>

## Seguridad digital {#digital}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Usa Signal** y asegúrate de que todo el mundo tenga [los mensajes temporales activados por defecto](/action/#signal-disappearing) para todos los chats nuevos.
  * **Make a plan to have [most phones off-site](/action/).**

    Anyone risking arrest should either (A) leave their phone at home or (B) power it down and give it to one person who collects phones and returns them after arrests. Anyone who still has a phone should turn it off if the risk of arrest goes up.
  * **Don’t [write things down](/action/#signal-disappearing)** that you wouldn’t want read back to you in court, even on Signal.
</InlineChecklist>

## Safety & de-escalation {#safety}

<InlineChecklist>

## Legal support & know your rights {#legal}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Find a movement-aligned lawyer and consult them about your action.**

    Places to look: your [local NLG chapter](https://www.nlg.org/chapters/), the [National Lawyers Guild](https://www.nlg.org/), [Movement Law Lab](https://www.movementlawlab.org/), or [CLDC](https://cldc.org/). See [questions to ask your attorney about legal risks](https://ccrjustice.org/sites/default/files/attach/2024/11/Protest%20Planner%202024.pdf).
  * **Hold a legal briefing** for your group with a movement-aligned lawyer, so everyone understands the range of risks and knows how to assert their rights.
  * **Find trained [Legal Observers](https://www.nlg.org/massdefenseprogram/los/)** to observe your action if needed.
</InlineChecklist>

## Arrests, jail support, & police liaison {#jail-support}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Recruit a [jail support](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yVVW7_N47PKp_OW5Ge7K2452vB9Ar5BIbTcFsZXRMC8/edit?tab=t.0) team** with at least one on-site and one off-site person.
  * **Collect jail support forms.**

    For coordinated, pre-planned actions, collect a form from everyone risking arrest ([Example 1](https://www.punchupcollective.org/faq-the-role-of-police-liaison/), [Example 2](https://www.thursdaybram.com/zines/jail%20support%20form.pdf), [Example 3](https://rosehipmedics.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Jail-Support-Form-Only-2-pages.pdf)). Paper is best, but [CryptPad](https://cryptpad.fr/) forms work if you need to go digital.
  * **Elige un número de teléfono al que la gente pueda llamar desde la cárcel si hace falta.**

    Puede ser una [línea de ayuda local del NLG](https://www.nlg.org/chapters/) (¡si ya lo acordaste con ellos!) o simplemente alguien de tu grupo que no esté en el lugar. Make sure each person writes it on their body with a Sharpie.
  * **Recruit a [police liaison](https://www.punchupcollective.org/faq-the-role-of-police-liaison/)**.
  * **Destruye la información de apoyo legal** después de la acción.
</InlineChecklist>

## Vetting, need-to-know, & security culture {#security-culture}

<InlineChecklist>
  * **Develop action agreements** ([Example 1](https://stopthemoneypipeline.org/how-we-act/), [Example 2](https://counteract.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/participants-agreement-sample-2023.pdf)).
  * **Crea un proceso de verificación o de [aval](https://www.thedirectactionmovement.com/vouching), en el que alguien de confianza responda por la persona.**
  * **Create need-to-know agreements.**

    Don’t share information with anyone outside your group who doesn’t need to know, and don’t talk about the action, before or after, with anyone who wasn’t involved.
  * **Practice solidarity [against state repression](https://nspm7union.com/defendants-pledge/).**

    Under this authoritarian state, names that colleagues shared have been used to expand surveillance, and jokes from Signal threads people handed over have been presented in court as serious threats. Don’t talk to the cops, don’t give them access to data or information, and don’t become a cooperating witness. Así es como nos cuidamos entre todes.
</InlineChecklist>
