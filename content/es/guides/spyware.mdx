Spyware is software that infects your device to spy on you and steal your data without your knowledge or consent. Phones are most often targeted because they record detailed location data, multi-factor (2FA) codes, and a great deal of other personal information, but laptops and other devices can be targeted as well.

Spyware can extract your private messages (even on encrypted messenger apps like Signal), access your 2FA codes, monitor your conversations, track your location in real time, and even turn on your camera or microphone without your awareness. Todos esos datos se envían a quien controle el spyware. It's like they are looking over your shoulder as you use your phone.

Spyware is made and sold by private companies to governments. It gets used not just against criminals but also against activists, journalists, and lawyers. Most recently, [ICE has purchased access to spyware tools](https://www.404media.co/were-suing-ice-for-its-2-million-spyware-contract/).

The two most well-known mobile spyware programs are Pegasus (from NSO Group) and Graphite (from Paragon Solutions). Ambas empresas fueron fundadas por exejecutivos de la Unidad 8200, el equivalente israelí de la NSA en Estados Unidos. But [newer](https://www.wired.com/story/coruna-iphone-hacking-toolkit-us-government/) [spyware](https://www.wired.com/story/hundreds-of-millions-of-iphones-can-be-hacked-with-a-new-tool-found-in-the-wild/) is being discovered all the time, and spyware is one corner of the larger world of "malware" and "stalkerware," which are also used by oppressive governments and by abusive partners to silence people.

## ¿Qué tan probable es que yo sea blanco de spyware?

While governments claim to use spyware only against criminals, there are many documented cases of it being deployed against political activists, journalists, and lawyers by governments:

* High-profile organizers and activists ([In Catalonia](https://citizenlab.ca/research/catalangate-extensive-mercenary-spyware-operation-against-catalans-using-pegasus-candiru/))
* Human rights lawyers ([In Mexico](https://citizenlab.ca/research/lawyers-murdered-women-nso-group/))
* Investigative journalists (See [Forbidden Stories](https://forbiddenstories.org/pegasus-the-new-global-weapon-for-silencing-journalists/) and [Amnesty International](https://securitylab.amnesty.org/case-study-the-pegasus-project))
* También hemos visto casos donde los dispositivos de familiares de las personas objetivo han sido infectados con spyware. (See[Citizen Lab](https://citizenlab.ca/research/reckless-exploit-mexico-nso/))

<Alert type="success">
  Spyware like Pegasus and Graphite costs governments **tens or hundreds of thousands** of dollars to deploy. **Therefore, the vast moajority of activists will **NOT** be targeted with spyware and would benefit more from [protecting against other threats](/essentials/).**
</Alert>

Sugerimos que empieces con nuestra [Lista de esenciales de seguridad](/essentials/) y luego regreses a esta guía. That guide will help you protect against cheaper-to-deploy surveillance technologies like location tracking or phone-cracking software police can use after they seize your device.

As repressive politicians continue to push the US towards authoritarianism, we should expect sophisticated spyware to be used more broadly against activists.

**The good news:** the steps you need to take to help protect yourself from spyware and stalkerware will also help you protect against other kinds of attacks that are more likely to affect you, so they are good to complete regardless.

<Section title="Analizar dispositivos" slug="testing">
  Si puedes usar un iPhone, puedes ejecutar un análisis de spyware con [iMazing](https://imazing.com/spyware-analyzer). Es gratis (no hagas clic en ninguno de los botones de "comprar"). Technical users can also run the [Mobile Verification Toolkit (MVT)](https://github.com/mvt-project/mvt) for detailed consensual device forensics.

  However, detecting spyware on a device is not a trivial task and you shouldn't rely on just anyone to test your phone. Civil society members globally have turned to these institutions to help test their phones and run detailed analysis with the support of experts:

  * [Citizen Lab](https://citizenlab.ca/spyware-outreach/) - los principales expertos en análisis de spyware
  * [Access Now](https://www.accessnow.org/help/)
  * [Amnesty Security Lab](https://securitylab.amnesty.org/get-help/)
  * [Constitutional Communications](https://constitutionalcommunications.org/) - ayudan a que el análisis de spyware llegue a más personas en comunidades activistas
  * [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://www.eff.org/about/contact) - para personas en Estados Unidos

  Cada organización tiene su propio proceso de verificación, y puede tardar un poco en responder a tu solicitud mientras confirman todo por su parte.

  ### Cómo darte cuenta de si podrías estar infectade

  El spyware normalmente no muestra síntomas notorios e infecta *en silencio*. Sin embargo, algunas quejas comunes de personas afectadas por spyware a lo largo de los años han incluido:

  * La batería del teléfono se agota rápidamente
  * Comportamiento sospechoso de ciertas apps
  * Uso anormal de datos
  * Recepción de enlaces o archivos adjuntos sospechosos

  <Alert type="warning">
    Si notas alguno de estos síntomas, no significa necesariamente que tu dispositivo haya sido hackeado con spyware. Te recomendamos contactar a alguna de las organizaciones mencionadas arriba para que analicen tu dispositivo.
  </Alert>

  <Alert type="info">
    El spyware es un malware muy avanzado que un antivirus común **no detectará**. Te recomendamos contactar a organizaciones expertas y confiables para que te apoyen a determinar si tu dispositivo muestra señales de haber sido comprometido (en el pasado o en el presente).
  </Alert>

  **Apple** y **WhatsApp** ahora notifican regularmente a las personas usuarias cuando sospechan que sus dispositivos han sido blanco de spyware mercenario. Aquí tienes más información sobre las [notificaciones de amenaza de Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102174) y las [notificaciones de amenaza de WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/641700318302674).
