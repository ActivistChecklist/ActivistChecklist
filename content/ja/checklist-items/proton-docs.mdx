Google tracks all user activity - including what documents you open, share, and access. Law enforcement can obtain this data through [subpoenas/warrants](term:warrant-subpoena), and they've been known to share it without proper authorization. Doing our organizing outside of Google Docs helps us keep each other more safe.

[**Proton Docs**](https://proton.me/drive/docs) is a high-quality alternative to Google Docs. Your docs are end-to-end encrypted, which means that even if the authorities accessed Proton's servers, they wouldn't be able to see what was contained in your documents.

<Alert type="warning">
  **Proton Docs does not make you anonymous by default!** If you are doing higher risk organizing, make sure to learn the [limitations of Proton and how to use it safely](/proton/).
</Alert>

<HowTo title="How to use Proton Docs">
  1. [Create a Proton account](https://proton.me/) if you don't have one already — choose the free plan
  2. <MenuPath inline>Visit [Proton Drive](http://drive.proton.me/) > Click the **New** button > Select **New Document**</MenuPath>
  3. Once your document is ready for sharing, you can click the "Share" button and either share it with other Proton accounts or enable "Create public link" to share with people who don't have a Proton account. Be careful where you share the link since it will be less secure! We recommend using a password if you're turning on "anyone with link can view" mode.
</HowTo>

Encourage the people you're organizing with to get a Proton account and you can all move toward being more secure.

**Looking for forms?** Proton doesn't have a solution like Google Forms. You can try [Cryptpad forms](https://cryptpad.fr/) or [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/) with the end-to-end encrypted option enabled.
