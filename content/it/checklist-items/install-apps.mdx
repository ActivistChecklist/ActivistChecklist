title: Installa un browser sicuro
titleBadges: []
---

* Installa [Onion Browser](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/onion-browser/id519296448) (iPhone) o [Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org/download/#android) (Android) per la massima privacy.
* O almeno installa [Brave Browser](https://brave.com/en-in/download/#brave-mobile) da usare al posto del browser predefinito.
