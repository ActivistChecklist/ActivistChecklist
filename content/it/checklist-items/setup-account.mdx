---
title: Crea un account Apple/Google separato per l'app store
---

<HowTo title="Come creare un account Apple/Google separato">
  <StepList>
    1. Create the account
       * For iPhone: [Apple ID instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108647)
       * For Android: [Google instructions](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27441?hl=en/\&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid\&oco=1)
    2. You will need a phone number to verify your new account. Thankfully, you should be able to receive texts on your protest phone now.
    3. Sign into the new account in your phone’s settings
    4. Visit the Apple App Store or Google Play Store to confirm that you’re now able to download apps
  </StepList>
</HowTo>

**Consigli**

* Non usare il tuo vero nome quando crei questo account.
