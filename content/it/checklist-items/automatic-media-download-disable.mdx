---
title: Turn off automatic media downloads on Signal and WhatsApp
---

When someone sends you a photo or video, your phone often downloads it automatically. Even though you haven't opened it, your device is processing that file to generate a thumbnail or preview. Some spyware attacks have been delivered through WhatsApp using images (or files) that exploit bugs in how your phone processes the images.

(Note: There has never been an example of Signal being exploited in the same way, but you can disable it in Signal as well if you want be very cautious.)

<HowTo title="How to disable automatic media downloads">
  **WhatsApp:**
  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* **Settings** > **Storage and Data**. Under **Media Auto-Download**, set **Photos**, **Audio**, **Video**, and **Documents** to **Never**.</MenuPath>
  <MenuPath>*Android:* Tap ⋮ > **Settings** > **Storage and data**. Under **Media auto-download**, tap **When using mobile data**, **When connected on Wi-Fi**, and **When roaming** one at a time. **Uncheck every media (photos, audio, videos, documents** type and tap **OK**.</MenuPath>

  **Signal:**
  <MenuPath>*iPhone:* Tap your profile picture > **Settings** > **Data Usage**. Set **Images**, **Audio**, **Video**, and **Documents** to **Never**.</MenuPath>
  <MenuPath>*Android:* Tap your profile picture > **Data and Storage**. Under **Media auto-download**, tap **When using mobile data**, **When using Wi-Fi**, and **When roaming** one at a time and **Uncheck every media (photos, audio, videos, documents**.</MenuPath>
</HowTo>
